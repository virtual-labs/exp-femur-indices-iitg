Aim: The aim of femur measurement is to assess the length and growth of the femur (thigh bone).Femur length helps estimate fetal gestational age and growth and is compared with other measurements such as head and abdominal measurements.In anatomy/anthropology femur measurement can help assess bone growth, body proportions, and estimated height. In orthopedics it helps evaluate bone length, deformities, fractures, and limb-length differences.
